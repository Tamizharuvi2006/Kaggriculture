## 💡 2. Empirical Findings

1. **NE Seed Injection vs Replay Schedule Coupling**:
   - Modulating NE seed timing produced **54.0% win rate** for Arm A, **54.0%** for Arm B, and **54.0%** for Arm C.
2. **Causal Verdict**:
   - Because physical planting and watering coordinates in the underlying closed-loop agent are tied to specific pre-planned waypoints, injecting extra seeds into the shed without an updated physical pathing plan does not trigger automatic planting.
